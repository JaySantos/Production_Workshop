# Acme Cloud Storage — Refund Policy

Acme Cloud Storage offers a 14-day money-back guarantee on all new paid
subscriptions. Refund requests must be submitted through the account
billing page within 14 calendar days of the initial charge.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear
within 5-10 business days, depending on the card issuer or bank.

Digital add-ons purchased separately from the base subscription (extra
storage blocks, priority support add-ons) are non-refundable once
provisioned, because the additional capacity is allocated immediately
and cannot be reclaimed.

Annual plans downgraded mid-cycle are not eligible for a prorated refund
of the unused period; customers can instead let the plan run out and
choose not to renew.

Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms specified in the signed
order form rather than this self-serve policy — contact your account
manager for enterprise refund requests.

## Free trials
Plans started via a free trial are not charged until the trial ends, so
there is nothing to refund during the trial period itself. If you forget
to cancel and are charged when the trial converts to paid, the standard
14-day money-back guarantee applies from the date of that first charge.

## Chargebacks
Customers are asked to contact Acme support and request a refund
directly rather than filing a chargeback with their card issuer.
Accounts with an open chargeback are suspended pending investigation,
and repeated chargebacks (rather than using the refund process) may
result in permanent account termination regardless of the outcome of
the chargeback itself.

## Promotional and discounted subscriptions
Subscriptions purchased at a promotional or discounted rate are still
eligible for the standard 14-day money-back guarantee, but the refunded
amount is the discounted price actually paid, not the plan's regular
list price.

## Referral credits
Referral credits applied to an account are not cash-equivalent and are
not refunded or paid out if a subscription is refunded or cancelled;
they are simply forfeited. If a subscription paid for partly with
referral credit and partly by card is refunded within the 14-day window,
only the card-paid portion is returned to the original payment method.

## Multiple refund requests on the same account
An account is limited to one money-back-guarantee refund per rolling
12-month period. A second refund request within that window will be
declined even if it otherwise falls within 14 days of a new charge.

## Taxes on refunds
Where sales tax, VAT, or GST was charged on the original purchase, the
tax amount is refunded along with the subscription price. Refunds are
processed as a single combined amount rather than separate line items.

## Cancelling without requesting a refund
You can cancel a subscription at any time without requesting a refund;
cancellation stops future billing but does not, by itself, refund any
already-charged period. Cancelled accounts retain access until the end
of the current paid billing period.

## Checking refund status
Submitted refund requests can be tracked from the billing page under
"Refund history," which shows a status of Pending, Approved, or
Declined, along with the date a refund was issued once processed.

## EU and UK statutory withdrawal rights
Customers in the EU and UK may have a statutory 14-day right of
withdrawal for online purchases under local consumer protection law,
separate from and in addition to Acme's own money-back guarantee;
starting to actively use significant storage or API access during that
period may affect eligibility for statutory withdrawal, consistent with
standard digital-service withdrawal exceptions.

## Refunds after a data deletion request
If you have requested full account and data deletion under Acme's
privacy policy, any refund owed is still issued to your original
payment method even though your account data itself will not be
retained; refund processing does not require restoring deleted data.
