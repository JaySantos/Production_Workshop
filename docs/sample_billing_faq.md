# Acme Cloud Storage — Billing FAQ

## What payment methods are accepted?
Acme accepts major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American
Express) for all plans. Business and Enterprise plans can also pay via
bank transfer (ACH or wire) or by purchase order, subject to credit
approval.

## What currencies are supported?
Billing is available in USD, EUR, and GBP. The currency shown at
checkout is based on your account's billing address, and once a
subscription is created its currency cannot be changed without
cancelling and re-subscribing.

## Do I get an invoice?
Yes. An invoice or receipt is automatically generated for every charge
and available under Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History as a
downloadable PDF. Business and Enterprise plans can add a company name,
tax ID, and billing address to appear on invoices.

## What happens if I upgrade mid-cycle?
Upgrading mid-cycle charges a prorated amount immediately, covering the
difference between your old and new plan for the remainder of the
current billing period. Your renewal date does not change.

## What happens if I downgrade mid-cycle?
Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle rather
than immediately, and there is no prorated credit for the unused
portion of the higher-tier plan in the current cycle.

## What happens if a payment fails?
Acme retries a failed payment automatically over the following 7 days.
During this grace period your plan remains active. If payment still
fails after 7 days, the account is downgraded to the free plan (storage
becomes read-only if it exceeds the free plan's limit) until a valid
payment method is added and the balance is settled.

## Is billing different for annual vs. monthly plans?
Annual billing is discounted compared to paying monthly (typically
equivalent to two months free), charged as a single upfront payment for
the year, and renews automatically at the end of the 12-month period
unless cancelled beforehand.

## How does billing work for Business team seats?
Business plans are billed per seat per month (or per year, if on annual
billing). Adding a seat mid-cycle charges a prorated amount for the
remainder of the current period; removing a seat takes effect at the
next renewal and does not issue a prorated refund for the current
period, consistent with the mid-cycle downgrade policy above.

## Is tax included in the displayed price?
Displayed prices exclude tax where applicable. VAT, GST, or applicable
sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your billing address and
shown as a separate line item before you confirm payment.

## Can Enterprise customers pay by purchase order?
Yes, subject to a credit check and a signed order form. Purchase-order
billing is invoiced according to the payment terms in that order form
rather than charged automatically to a card.

## Who can view or change billing information?
Only the account owner and users explicitly granted the "Billing admin"
role can view invoices, change the payment method, or modify the
subscription plan; regular team members cannot access billing settings
even if they have workspace admin permissions.
